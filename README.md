# Unit 2 Chem Trainer

A study app for an Honors Chemistry Unit 2 test: **Module 6** (ionic compounds and metals), **Module 7** (covalent bonding), **Module 9** (the mole) and the **types of bonding lab**.

**Open it:** https://georgehsalib.github.io/unit2-chem-trainer/

## What's in it
- **Practice:** randomized questions with worked steps, in multiple choice, written or mixed style. Questions you miss come back more often.
- **Flashcards:** vocabulary for all three modules, polyatomic ions, prefixes, shapes, mole steps and ion charges.
- **Mock test:** 20, 30 or 45 questions, then a score, letter grade, breakdown by lesson and every answer explained.
- **Reference:** periodic table, ions, naming rules, VSEPR, electronegativity, the lab's bond-type table and the mole road map.
- **Tools:** molar mass and percent composition calculator (handles `Ca3(PO4)2` and `CuSO4·5H2O`), mole converter, and empirical/molecular formula solver.
- **Progress:** accuracy per lesson, your weakest question types and your best mock test.

One self-contained `index.html`, no build step. Works offline once loaded, on phones and desktops, in light and dark mode. Progress is saved only in your own browser.
